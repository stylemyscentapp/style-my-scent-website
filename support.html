We’re here to help

Support

For help, email stylemyscentapp@gmail.com. Include your phone model, Android or iPhone, app version, and what happened. Never send your password.

Bottle identification

Photograph the front label in good light with the brand and fragrance name visible. Confirm the exact concentration or flanker before saving if the app presents possible matches.

Collection and autocomplete

Choose Fragrance, Oil, Lotion, or Mist before searching. You can use manual entry if the correct product is not available. Uncertain catalog identities are held for review rather than guessed.

Cloud sync

The app is local-first. If sync is temporarily unavailable, your on-device Collection can remain available. Confirm you are signed into the intended account, reconnect, and retry.

Deals

Prices and inventory can change. Tester listings are labeled and remain eligible when they match the canonical fragrance. Purchases and returns are handled by the retailer.

Notifications

Review reminders require notification permission. You can change notification access in your device settings.

Account deletion

Delete your cloud account inside the app or follow the steps on the Delete Account page.
