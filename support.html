Style My Scent Support

Need help with Style My Scent? We’re happy to help.

Contact

Email: stylemyscentapp@gmail.com

When reporting a problem, include your phone model, whether you use Android or iPhone, and a short description of what happened. Please do not send passwords.

Cloud backup

Style My Scent is designed to keep a phone copy of your fragrance data even when cloud backup is unavailable. If cloud backup shows an issue, your on-device collection can still remain available while you retry syncing.

Bottle identification

For best results, photograph the front label in good light and keep the bottle name and brand visible. If Style My Scent is not certain about the exact version, review the suggested identity before saving it.

Deals

Deal links may open third-party retailer websites. Prices, inventory and retailer information can change after Style My Scent checks them. Tester bottles may be shown when they are the same fragrance identity.

Account deletion

You can permanently delete a Style My Scent cloud account from the account panel inside the app. If you cannot access the app, visit our account deletion page.
