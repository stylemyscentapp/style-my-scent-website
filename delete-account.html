Account controls

Delete Account

You can permanently delete your Style My Scent cloud account and its associated cloud-backed data.

Delete in the app

  1. Open Style My Scent.
  2. Open the Cloud account panel.
  3. Choose Delete Cloud Account.
  4. Review the warning and choose Delete My Cloud Account.

What is deleted

  • Your authentication account.
  • Cloud-backed Collection and wishlist records.
  • Cloud-backed journal entries and saved scent combinations.
  • Cloud-backed preferences.

Device-local data

Because Style My Scent is local-first, deleting the cloud account does not automatically erase copies stored locally on a device you control. Remove local entries in the app or uninstall/reset the app if you also want to clear device-local storage.

If you cannot access the app

Email from the address associated with your account. We may ask you to verify account ownership before deletion.
