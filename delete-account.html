Delete Your Style My Scent Account

Style My Scent gives you control over your cloud account and the data backed up to it.

Delete directly in the app

Open Style My Scent, tap ☁️ Cloud on, choose DELETE CLOUD ACCOUNT, review the confirmation, and choose DELETE MY CLOUD ACCOUNT.

This permanently removes your Style My Scent cloud login and the collection, wishlist, journal, saved-combo and preference data associated with that cloud account.

Request deletion without the app

If you cannot access the app, you can request deletion from the email address associated with your Style My Scent account.

Email an account deletion request

We may ask you to verify ownership of the account before processing a request sent outside the app.

What is deleted

  • Your Style My Scent authentication account.
  • Cloud-backed collection and wishlist records.
  • Cloud-backed journal entries and saved scent combinations.
  • Cloud-backed Addison preferences.

What stays on your phone

Style My Scent is local-first. Deleting your cloud account does not automatically erase the copy of your fragrance data stored locally on a device you control. You can remove local entries from the app itself.
