What Style My Scent does

Style My Scent helps people identify fragrance bottles, manage a fragrance collection and wishlist, keep a scent journal, receive styling suggestions, and find supported retailer offers.

Information you provide

  • Account information: email address and authentication information when you create a cloud account.
  • Collection content: saved fragrances, oils, lotions, mists, wishlist items, journal entries, combinations, preferences, and related notes.
  • Photos: camera or library images you choose for bottle identification or a saved Collection item.
  • Support messages: information you include when contacting support.

Camera and bottle identification

Camera and photo-library access is requested only when you choose a photo feature. A selected bottle photo may be sent to the secure bottle-identification service and its AI provider to identify visible label details. If a stock image is unavailable, your chosen photo may be displayed temporarily in the app. Device permission can be changed in system settings.

Local storage and cloud sync

The app is local-first. Core data can remain on your device. If you create an account and enable cloud sync, supported Collection, wishlist, journal, saved-combination, and preference data is stored with Supabase so it can be restored or synchronized. Do not store sensitive personal information in free-text fields.

Deals and affiliate links

Deals may contain third-party retailer and affiliate links. When you open one, the retailer and affiliate network may receive ordinary visit and attribution information under their own policies. Style My Scent does not process retailer payments.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Style My Scent may schedule device notifications such as a later reminder to review a scent combination. You can disable notifications in device settings.

Premium subscriptions

If Premium subscriptions are offered, purchases and subscription management are handled by Apple or Google. Style My Scent may receive purchase status needed to unlock features, but does not receive your full payment-card details. Service providers

Supabase provides authentication, database, storage, and backend functions. AI services may process bottle-identification requests. Retailers and affiliate networks process outbound shopping activity after you leave the app. Apple and Google may process store purchases and diagnostics according to their policies.

Retention and security

Cloud data is retained while your account is active and as reasonably needed to provide the service, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, and prevent abuse.
