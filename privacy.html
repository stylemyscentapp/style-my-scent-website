Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 11, 2026

What Style My Scent does

Style My Scent helps people identify fragrance bottles, manage a fragrance collection and wishlist, keep a scent journal, receive styling suggestions, and find supported retailer offers.

Information you provide

  • Account information: email address and authentication information when you create an optional cloud account.
  • Collection content: saved fragrances, oils, lotions, mists, wishlist items, journal entries, combinations, preferences, and related notes.
  • Photos: camera or library images you choose for bottle identification or a saved Collection item.
  • Support messages: information you include when contacting support.

Camera and bottle identification

Camera and photo-library access is requested only when you choose a photo feature. A selected bottle photo may be sent to the secure bottle-identification service and its AI provider to identify visible label details. If a stock image is unavailable, your chosen photo may be displayed in the app. Device permission can be changed in system settings.

Local storage and cloud sync

The app is local-first. Core data can remain on your device. If you create an account and use cloud sync, supported Collection, wishlist, journal, saved-combination, and preference data is stored with Supabase so it can be restored or synchronized. Account-owned cloud records are protected by per-user access controls. Do not store sensitive personal information in free-text fields.

Deals and affiliate links

Deals may contain third-party retailer and affiliate links. When you open one, the retailer and affiliate network may receive ordinary visit and attribution information under their own policies. Style My Scent does not process retailer payments. See the Affiliate Disclosure.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Style My Scent may schedule device notifications such as a later reminder to review a scent combination. You can disable notifications in device settings.

Paid features

Paid Premium features are not currently offered for purchase. If paid features are introduced, the purchase terms and this policy will be updated as needed before those products go live. Store purchases would be processed by Apple or Google rather than by Style My Scent directly.

Service providers

Supabase provides authentication, database, storage, and backend functions. AI services may process bottle-identification requests. Retailers and affiliate networks process outbound shopping activity after you leave the app. Apple and Google may process app-store activity according to their policies.

Retention and security

Cloud account data is retained while your account is active and as reasonably needed to provide the service. When an in-app cloud-account deletion completes successfully, the authentication account and account-owned active cloud records are deleted. Limited technical, security, fraud-prevention, legal, or backup records may remain for a limited period when required by law or by provider infrastructure. Device-local copies remain under your control and are not automatically erased by cloud-account deletion. Reasonable safeguards are used, but no system can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

You may use core local features without cloud sync, change camera/photo/notification permissions, edit or delete saved items, sign out, and delete your cloud account. See Delete Account.

Children

Style My Scent is not directed to children under 13. If the law where you live requires a higher minimum age or parental consent for an online service, use Style My Scent only when those requirements are met.

Contact and updates

Style My Scent is independently operated. Questions or privacy requests: stylemyscentapp@gmail.com. Material policy changes will be reflected here with an updated date.
