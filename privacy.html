Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2, 2026

Style My Scent is a fragrance styling, collection, journal, wishlist and shopping app. This policy describes the information used by the current app features and the Style My Scent website.

Information you choose to provide

  • Account information. If you create a Style My Scent account, your email address and authentication information are processed through our cloud authentication provider.
  • Your fragrance data. Collection items, favorites, wishlist items, scent-journal entries, saved recipes and Addison preferences can be backed up to your account when cloud backup is enabled.
  • Bottle photos. If you use photo identification, the image you choose or take is sent to the bottle-identification service so the app can suggest a fragrance match. A copy of a photo you save with a Collection item may also remain on your device.

How information is used

We use this information to provide the features you request, including restoring your fragrance wardrobe, generating collection-based styling suggestions, keeping your journal and wishlist, identifying bottles, and syncing supported account data between devices.

Service providers

Style My Scent uses service providers to operate parts of the app. Supabase provides cloud authentication, database and backend-function infrastructure. Bottle-identification requests may also be processed by an AI service used by our secure backend. These providers process information as needed to provide those services.

Deals and retailer links

Style My Scent may show links to third-party retailers and affiliate offers. If you open a retailer link, that retailer and its advertising or affiliate partners may receive the normal information associated with visiting their site. Their own privacy policies apply after you leave Style My Scent.

Website signup

The email field on the current early-access website is still a demonstration form and is not connected to a mailing-list service. It does not submit the entered address to Style My Scent.

Your choices

You can use core on-device features without signing into cloud backup. You can also sign out while keeping the phone copy of your app data.

Account deletion

If you create a cloud account, you can initiate permanent deletion inside Style My Scent from the cloud-account panel. Account deletion removes the authentication account and its cloud-backed collection, wishlist, journal, saved-combo and preference records. Because Style My Scent is local-first, deleting the cloud account does not automatically erase a device-local copy that remains under your control.

If you cannot access the app, visit the Style My Scent account deletion page to request deletion.

Updates

We will update this policy as app features, service providers, marketing tools or retailer integrations materially change.

Questions, support or privacy requests: stylemyscentapp@gmail.com
